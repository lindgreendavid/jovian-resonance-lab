What holds Jupiter’s moons in rhythm?

Io, Europa, and Ganymede do not simply orbit at nearly 4:2:1. Their three mean longitudes combine into a Laplace angle that stays close to 180°. This lab asks which minimum physics can reproduce that behavior.

Confirmatory result

The expected ordering passed. The numerical warning also matters.

G4J2 had lower RMSE than K2, G3, and G4. Yet halving the step from 0.01 to 0.005 day changed its thirty-year trace by 8.13° RMSE. Exploratory halvings reduced successive differences to 2.15° and 0.54°, consistent with second-order convergence; the finest run reached 0.46° RMSE against JUP365. That finer result is promising, not confirmatory. The frozen ranking is supported; the nominal trajectory is not a precision ephemeris.

Stop at the boundary

What this study does not establish.

Not raw truth

JUP365 is a fitted and propagated ephemeris, not raw astrometry or a probability distribution.

Not formation history

A 30-year adequacy comparison cannot identify how the resonance formed or persisted over billions of years.

Not an ocean detector

No claim follows about tidal Q, interior heating, subsurface oceans, or habitability.

Not a universal ranking

G4J2’s win belongs to these models, states, frame choices, interval, and endpoint.
