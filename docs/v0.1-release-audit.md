# v0.1 release audit

## Scope

This audit covers research product v0.1.0 and the unchanged frozen v0.1 protocol. Version 0.1.0 is
the first complete public research release, not a claim of mature precision ephemeris software.

## Scientific identity

- Frozen protocol commit: `7d562ebaaf0be7073d3cfef206803472cbfce5e1`
- JUP365 daily samples: 10,957
- Interval: 2001-01-01 through 2030-12-31 TDB, inclusive
- Normalized reference SHA-256:
  `4eda38e1899306a9cfff6d6474748a3c8270dd20d63c72a0df4d0422f427d1fb`
- Response-reported source: `JUP365_MERGED`
- Primary model definitions and endpoint: unchanged after freeze

## Reproduction and consistency

- The extraction scripts reject source mismatch, missing rows, duplicates, and epoch misalignment.
- The report contains all four model traces and all frozen comparisons.
- README, research report, website, and machine report agree on sample count and primary metrics.
- The 12-hour cadence and ICRF-frame sensitivity are published separately from the primary result.
- The post-confirmatory convergence extension is visibly labelled and does not replace the frozen
  nominal run.

## Deviations and corrections

One report-serialization variable error was corrected after the first model execution; it occurred
after endpoint calculation and before any report was written. Daily mean-longitude alias handling
and exploratory convergence were added as transparent post-confirmatory clarifications. The exact
scope and non-effect on the primary endpoint are documented in `post-freeze-corrections.md`.

## Remaining limits

The nominal G4J2 trace is not numerically converged over thirty years. JUP365 is a fitted ephemeris,
and the model omits solar forcing, higher Jovian harmonics, relativity, tides, and pole precession.
No release language may turn this comparison into a claim about raw observations, causal formation,
billion-year stability, oceans, or habitability.
